I’m a designer who cares a lot.

I care about the details, how things feel to use, and the little moments that can make a product feel delightful. A lot of my work is about taking complex problems and turning them into experiences that feel simple, thoughtful, and easy to use.

Currently, I’m a Senior Product Designer at Delivery Hero, working on adtech products with some really talented people. Before that, I led design for business banking at Kuda and worked across logistics and transportation at MAX and Fieldinsight.

Working across different industries has shaped how I think about design. It’s taught me when to zoom out and think about the bigger picture, and when to get into the details that make an experience feel just right. Ultimately, I want to make things that are useful, well considered, and genuinely nice to use.

My background is in Computer Science, which has also shaped the way I think about products and how they’re built.

These days, I live in Berlin. Outside of work, I collect vinyl records, take pictures of things I find interesting, write, play sports, and play a lot of video games.
